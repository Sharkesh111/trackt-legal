Trackt Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Trackt is an app for talking about music: you rank albums, rate songs, play music games, and discuss lyric lines, songs and albums with other people. This policy explains what Trackt collects, why, who else handles it, and how to delete it. Trackt is run by Eshar Hans (“we”). Questions: trackt.app.support@gmail.com.

What we collect

Your account

  • Email address and password (the password is stored only as a hash by our auth provider), or your Apple ID token if you use Sign in with Apple (Apple may give us a private relay email).
  • Date of birth, asked once when you sign up. We use it to keep under-13s out and to work out your age bracket. It is not shown to anyone.
  • Your username (handle), and anything you choose to add to your profile: display name, bio, profile photo, school, favourite artist and album, and links to Instagram, TikTok, X or a website.

What you post and do in the app

  • Takes (short posts), comments and replies, photos and GIFs you attach, reactions and upvotes.
  • Album rankings, song ratings, saved albums, artists you like, who you follow, people you block or hide, and reports you send.
  • Game play: your games, answers, scores, streaks, pools you build and hearts.
  • These are public in the app where the feature is public (your profile, posts, comments and rankings), and private where it is private (hides, blocks, reports, game history).

Your contacts, only if you ask

  • If you tap Find people from your contacts, your phone turns each phone number and email into a one-way code (a SHA-256 hash) and sends only those codes. We compare them with the codes of people already on Trackt and keep nothing from the request. Contact names never leave your phone. You can turn off being found this way in Settings.

Device and safety information

  • A random ID for this install and the network address of a contacts request, both stored only as one-way hashes, to limit spam (how many posts or contact lookups one phone can make).
  • Crash reports, if crash reporting is on: what went wrong in the app, the app version, device model and system version, and an anonymous ID (a one-way code made from your account ID). No email, username, IP address, screenshots or what you typed.

We do not collect your location, your listening history from other apps, or your contacts’ names. We do not sell your data and we do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Why we use it

To run your account and the app’s features; to show your posts, rankings and profile to other people as the app is designed to; to keep the app safe (filtering slurs, scanning uploaded photos and GIFs for explicit content, rate limits, reports and blocks); and to find and fix crashes.

Who else handles it

  • Supabase hosts our database, sign-in and file storage.
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple; song previews stream from Apple’s iTunes service when you press play (Apple sees the request, not who you are on Trackt).
  • GIPHY: when you search for a GIF, our server sends your search words to GIPHY. Your account is never sent.
  • Sightengine scans photos and GIFs you upload for nudity, gore and images of minors before they’re stored. The image is not kept by them for us.
  • Sentry receives crash reports (see above), once crash reporting is switched on.

How long we keep it

As long as your account exists. Spam-limit records are kept for up to a few days. Crash reports expire on Sentry’s retention schedule. Backups of the database are encrypted and replaced on a rolling basis.

Deleting your account

Settings > Delete account. This permanently deletes your profile, ratings, rankings, posts, comments, game scores and hearts (including any you bought), your uploaded photos and GIFs, and your sign-in. Comments that someone else replied to stay as “[deleted]” from “Deleted user”, so other people’s replies still make sense; your name and words are removed. If you used Sign in with Apple, we also ask Apple to revoke Trackt’s access. Copies in encrypted backups age out.

Children

Trackt is not for children under 13. Sign-up asks for a date of birth and stops anyone under 13.

Your choices

You can edit or delete your posts, change your profile, turn off being found from contacts, block people, and delete your account at any time in the app. For anything else, email trackt.app.support@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes we’ll update the date above and, for a significant change, tell you in the app.
