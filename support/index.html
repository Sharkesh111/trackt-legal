Common questions

How do I delete my account?

Settings (the gear on your profile) > Delete account, type DELETE, then tap Delete account. It’s permanent. Everything tied to your account is removed; comments other people replied to stay as “[deleted]” so their replies still make sense.

How do I report a post, comment or person?

Tap ⋯ on the post or comment and choose Report, or open the person’s profile, tap ⋯ and choose Report account. Reported content is hidden from you straight away, and we review every report.

How do I block someone?

Their profile > ⋯ > Block. You won’t see each other anywhere in the app. Undo it in Settings > Blocked accounts.

I forgot my password.

On the sign-in screen tap “Forgot password?” and we’ll email you a link.

Where do the song clips come from?

Previews are streamed from Apple Music (iTunes). Each clip shows Apple Music’s badge and links to the song.

Why can’t I see the full lyrics?

Trackt shows the lines people are discussing. Full lyrics need a licence we don’t have yet.

Something’s broken.

Email trackt.app.support@gmail.com with what you were doing, your phone model, and the app version (Settings, at the bottom: “Trackt 1.0.0 (build N)”).
